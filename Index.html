FAQ

Is my data stored?

Only in your browser, and it resets each day. Forecast numbers and your assistant questions go to the AI service through your own server.

How accurate is it?

It uses population averages, so treat it as a planning guide. Individual responses vary.

Is this medical advice?

No. For medication or health concerns, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

What does "What if" do?

It draws a green dotted line showing how tomorrow would change if you slept a different amount.

Wellness forecasting, not medical advice. Age and caffeine effects use population averages, so real responses vary. Nights you haven't logged are treated as typical sleep. Your forecast numbers and the questions you ask the assistant are sent to the AI service through your own server.
