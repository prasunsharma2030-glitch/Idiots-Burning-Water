A closer look at your forecast

How Moodcast works

Moodcast takes a handful of everyday signals, turns them into a simple picture of how your day may unfold, and then helps you decide when to focus, slow down, or change course.

Scroll to explore
01
Log

Start with what actually happened.

Moodcast begins with a few things you already know: how long you slept, how active you were, how many hours you expect to work, how much time you spent on screens, and when you had drinks with caffeine.

Those inputs give the forecast a daily context instead of treating every day as identical. A logged night can shape tomorrow differently from a night that was shorter, busier, or more interrupted.

Plan

Use the forecast to shape the day.

The goal is not to control every hour. The AI score and hourly curve are there to help you put demanding things in places where they fit better. Moodcast can surface your strongest focus window, point out an expected dip, and show how choices such as sleep or later caffeine may change the curve.

That gives you a practical starting point: protect the peak for hard work, make lower-energy periods lighter, and use the “What if” view to test a different sleep scenario before you change your real schedule.

One important detail

The forecast is a guide, not a measurement of your body.

Age and caffeine effects use population averages, so real responses vary from person to person. Nights you have not logged are treated as typical sleep, which keeps the forecast moving without pretending missing data is known.

Questions

FAQ

Is my data stored?

Your forecast numbers and the questions you ask the assistant are sent to the AI service through your own server. The app also uses your saved forecast settings so the experience can continue across the page.

How accurate is it?

Think of the forecast as a pattern-based estimate rather than a precise measurement. It uses population averages for age and caffeine effects, and real responses vary. Missing nights are treated as typical sleep rather than guessed individually.

Is this medical advice?

No. Moodcast is a wellness forecasting tool, not medical advice. Its purpose is to help you notice patterns and plan your day, not diagnose or treat a condition.

What does “What if” do?

“What if” lets you explore a different sleep amount and compare the resulting forecast with your current one. It is a simple way to see how a change could shift the next day before you actually make that change.
